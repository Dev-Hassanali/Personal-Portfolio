The Story

Designer, systems-thinker, shipper

I believe good design is invisible until it’s missing. My job is to make complex software feel inevitable — for users, and for the teams building it.

Over 5+ years I’ve moved between worlds: agency speed, startup ambiguity, and enterprise-grade SaaS rigor. That range taught me the full arc — from a founder’s napkin sketch to a design system serving nine squads, from first user interview to the metric that moved after launch.

Today I focus on the frontier most designers are still watching from a distance: AI-native products. Copilots, agents, generative flows — interfaces where trust, latency and error states are the real design material. I prototype fast, write clear specs, speak engineer, and treat every handoff like a product of its own.
